FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to know anything about websites? ▼
Not at all. I handle everything technical. You just need to tell me about your business, send me some photos and text, and I'll take care of the rest. After launch, I'll explain how to contact me for updates.
Why is hosting free? Is there a catch? ▼
No catch. Static websites (which is what I build) can be hosted for free on platforms like GitHub Pages and Netlify. Because there's no database or server-side code, these platforms offer free tier hosting. The only cost is your custom domain name (optional), which you pay directly to a registrar like Namecheap — around ₱800–₱1,200 per year.
What if I want to update my menu prices or photos later? ▼
For the first 30 days after launch, minor text and photo updates are free. After that, you can add an annual maintenance plan (₱800/year) for ongoing updates. Alternatively, you can always reach out and I'll give you a small per-update rate.
Can you add online ordering or a booking system? ▼
Currently, I focus on static websites — meaning no complex backend features like live ordering, payment processing, or user accounts. However, I can integrate simple solutions like a contact form that sends emails, a "Call to Order" button, or a WhatsApp/Messenger chat link that works great for most small businesses.
How do I pay? ▼
50% downpayment to start, 50% upon delivery and your approval. Payment via GCash, Maya, or bank transfer. No payment is collected until we've agreed on the scope and you're comfortable moving forward.
Are you based in Calamba? Can we meet in person? ▼
Yes, I'm based in Purok 4, Asia 2, Brgy. Canlubang, Calamba City, Laguna. I'm happy to meet in person for clients in Calamba and nearby areas (Los Baños, Sta. Rosa, Biñan, San Pedro). For clients farther away, we can work entirely through Messenger or email.
