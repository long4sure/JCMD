The Artist

Born in
Calamba.
Built to feel.

Long Live is the solo project of a Calamba-based indie artist whose music lives somewhere between late-night honesty and bedroom pop. Drawing from personal experience, restless nights, and the quiet weight of growing up, his songs speak to the part of you that stays awake at 2AM thinking about everything.

With a sound rooted in Alt-R&B, indie folk, and lo-fi pop, Long Live has been quietly building a following through small shows, raw recordings, and music that refuses to be anything other than real.

Started releasing in 2020. Still going. Still feeling it.
