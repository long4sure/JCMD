Our Story

More than a coffee shop.
A third place.

Cortado Coffee started as a small corner cart in 2021 with one espresso machine and a big dream. Today, we're a full café where locals come to slow down — to work, to meet, to just exist for a while.

We source our beans from small farms in Benguet and Sagada, roasted weekly so every cup you get is as fresh as possible. Our baristas are trained to care — not just to make drinks, but to make your day a little better.
